n/p Ratio

The plots below show the gain in ROC AUC from augmentation as a function of the dataset’s n/p ratio before augmentation (the ratio of number of samples to number of features). Each tab corresponds to a single augmentation factor. Gain is defined as follows:

\[ \mathrm{ROC\; AUC\; Gain} = \frac{1}{20}\sum_{i=1}^{20} \left(\mathrm{AUC}^{\text{test}}_{\text{aug},\,i} - \mathrm{AUC}^{\text{test}}_{\text{bench},\,i}\right) \]

Gain in the AUC is the test-set ROC AUC of the model trained on augmented data minus test-set ROC AUC of its corresponding benchmark, averaged over 20 train-test splits.
